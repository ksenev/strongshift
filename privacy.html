Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

Strongshift builds training plans that fit around shift work. To do that we handle information about your work roster and your training — including health information. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have. We're based in Queensland, Australia, and we handle personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

The short version: your training data is used to build your plan and nothing else. We don't sell it, we don't advertise against it, and we never share it with employers or insurers. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address.
  • Training data you enter — roster and shift times, training goals, your workout log, weights, reps and sets, run distances and times.
  • Optional extras — a profile photo, and GPS route data for a workout if you choose to sync it from a wearable.
  • Roster uploads — a photo, PDF or spreadsheet of your roster, if you upload one.
  • Technical data — app version, device type and crash diagnostics.

Sensitive information

Fitness and workout data is health information under the Australian Privacy Principles, which gives it extra protection. We collect it only with your consent, and only to generate and track your training plan. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not disclose it to employers, insurers, or data brokers.

How we use it

To build a plan that works around your roster, track your progress and streaks, run your account and subscription, respond to your support messages, and diagnose faults.

When you upload a roster, the image or file is sent to Anthropic's Claude API purely to transcribe the shifts into dates and shift codes. It is not used to train Anthropic's models, and we don't keep the file after transcription — only the resulting shift codes.

Where it's stored, and who else touches it

Your data lives on your device and in our database. We use a small number of processors who handle data on our behalf, under contract:

  • Supabase — database and account hosting.
  • Anthropic — roster transcription only, as described above.
  • Apple and RevenueCat — subscription processing. We never see your payment details.

Some of these providers store or process data outside Australia. Access to your records is restricted to your own account by database-level security rules.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account we erase your data from our live systems immediately; residual copies in encrypted backups are purged on the normal backup rotation.

Your choices and rights

  • View and edit your data at any time inside the app.
  • Delete your account from Profile — this permanently erases your account, roster, plan and history from our servers.
  • Turn notifications off in Profile, or in your device settings.
  • Request access to, or correction of, your information — or a copy of it — by emailing us.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Your login session is stored in your device's secure keychain. Database access is restricted per-account. No system is perfectly secure, but we'll notify you and the regulator as required if a breach ever puts you at serious risk.

Children

Strongshift isn't intended for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we make a material change we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Contact and complaints

Email hello@strongshift.app. If you think we've mishandled your information we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
