Common questions

How do I add my roster?

Open the Roster tab and tap a day to cycle it through Day, Late, Night and Off. If your pattern repeats, use the repeat-week or repeat-fortnight buttons. You can also tap Upload roster to send a photo, PDF or spreadsheet and have the shifts read automatically.

My roster changed. Do I have to rebuild the plan?

No. Change the shifts and the plan reshuffles itself — sessions move to days you can actually train, and post-night days stay protected for sleep.

The roster upload got a shift wrong.

Tap any day in the Roster tab to correct it. If a whole roster reads badly, a clearer, flatter photo in good light usually fixes it — and email me the file so I can improve it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they're cancelled through Apple — not from inside Strongshift. On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Strongshift → Cancel. Or open your App Store subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

I paid, but the app still shows a paywall.

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. If that doesn't work, email me with the date of purchase and I'll sort it out.

I want a refund.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something about the app caused the problem, tell me too — I'd rather fix it.

How do I get my data onto a new phone?

Install Strongshift and sign in with the same email and password. Your roster, plan and history come back from your account.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password? — you'll get a reset link.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. This permanently erases your account, roster, plan and workout history from your device and from our servers. It can't be undone.

Is Strongshift medical advice?

No. Strongshift provides general training and wellness guidance only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a qualified health professional before starting or changing an exercise programme, and stop and seek attention if you feel unwell or experience pain.
